## Needs a logic change (not done; listed)

- **PointFeedbackAnimation motion** (DS s.9): the chip enters with `scale 0.5 -> 1` and `y 10 -> -30`, leaves with `y -80, scale 0.8`, 0.8 s each way - more than 8 px, below scale 0.98, and up to 1.6 s for the two phases. These are framer-motion values in JS, not classes. It plays once, does not loop or bounce, and is skippable by doing nothing. Changing it = editing the `initial / animate / exit / transition` objects.
- Other JS motion values over the s.9 limits, same reason: `StudentActionPopover` panel slide `x: 12`; `TeacherDashboard` entrance `y: 20`, `x: +-20`, `scale: 0.95`; the HUD meter animates `width`; the skill panel `scale: 0.95`. (`Layout` wraps the page in `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`.)
- **Clickable `div`s with no keyboard path**: the student card in the live grid, the agenda row on the dashboard, the session row in `AttendanceMarking`. They need `role`, `tabIndex` and a key handler.
- **Log Spend dialog** (DS s.14): one red "Deduct N" button and the close cross; a named safe button ("Keep the cash") needs a handler. Title and button text unchanged.
- **HUD chip label** is the last word of the name (`name.split(" ").slice(-1)[0]`): two children called "Nguyên" get the same chip (the fixture has both). The full name is in the popover and wraps. A different label is a JS change.
- **Skill panel position** is computed in JS (`PANEL_H_ESTIMATE = 280`, corner picking, inline `position: fixed; zIndex: 40`). At 375 the assistant launcher (shell, bottom right) covers part of the panel's last tile ("Correction"); the bulk bar is clear of it.
