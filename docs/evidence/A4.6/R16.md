## What changed (ivory branch / hec classes)
- **StudentActionPopover**: the aurora band, gradients, glows and hover-scale are gone. Identity band is sunken and the whole name wraps. Today / month points are ink and tabular, with a brass bolt. Attendance chip: leaf / warn / danger / neutral, each with a word and an icon. Present / Late / Absent are 44 px tinted buttons. Action and skill tiles are 44 px surface tiles; corrections are 44 px danger panels. Flag types are 44 px pills with `aria-pressed`; the selected one is ink. The popover is named "Actions for <name>". Handlers and mutations are untouched.
- **LiveEngagementHUD**: no gradient card and no `animate-ping` loop. Chips are 44 px: leaf tint (engaged), surface (warm), warn tint (quiet), dashed (none), sunken struck-through (absent), points tabular. The meter is leaf / warn / danger on a sunken track. Badges are `success` / `warning`; the quiet line is warn and 14 px.
- **RadialSkillMenu / SkillButton**: a solid raised panel (no glass) with four 56 px tiles per row. Leaf = award, danger = correction, sunken = RT. The point popover has 44 px buttons and a 44 px custom input, a named confirm ("Give the custom points for X") and is named "Points for X". Reasons are 44 px danger outlines and wrap, 3 across from `sm`. The deduction buttons are 56 px.
- **LiveAssessmentGrid**: sunken toolbar; the selected count is a brass badge. Skill panel: one raised frame (the menu's own frame is dropped inside it), the full name wraps, 44 px close. The panel is capped at the 280 px the positioning code assumes and scrolls inside, with bottom clearance below `sm` for the floating tools button. Position, drag and Escape logic are untouched.
- **AssessmentStudentCard**: a surface card; the whole name wraps (it was `line-clamp-1`). Absent / Excused are badges with words; the selected card has a brass edge; points are a leaf / danger / neutral tabular badge. No gradient avatar ring, no blur, no hover lift.
- **PointFeedbackAnimation**: leaf / danger-fill pill, tabular. The motion is unchanged.
- **EconomyActions**: rows are warn panels; the name wraps. Approve, reject and log-spend are 44 px icon buttons labelled with the student's name. The log-spend dialog title wraps and cash is tabular.
- **BulkPointsDialog / ManualPointsDialog**: labels above fields; the selects and amount are linked to their labels. Category and skill choices are 44 px with a brass selection and `aria-pressed`. Quick points use leaf / danger icons with tabular amounts. The sheet uses `dvh` and categories are 3 across in the 480 px sheet. The selected-students well is taller.
- **ReadingTheoryScoreEntry**: below `sm` each name gets its own line above Current / New / Diff. The name was cut to one letter at 375; now it is never cut. Score inputs are 44 px and labelled "New score for X". The diff is leaf / danger, the summary is a neutral panel, and Save is the primary button (no teal).
- **AttendanceMarking**: state badges are 44 px leaf / danger / warn. The notices are warn / neutral panels. Names and class names wrap; times are tabular.
- **TeacherDashboard**: plain cards (no glow blobs, gradients or hover lift). The name is the serif display and wraps. Stats are sans h1 tabular. The pending dot is static warn (it was `animate-ping`). In the agenda, the lesson in progress has a brass edge with a brass "In Progress" badge (it was `animate-pulse`), and Held is a `success` badge. Class names wrap. Quick-action and class cards are focus-ringed links. The Tabs overrides are dropped. The entrance moves 8 px over 240 ms (it was 20 px over 500 ms). The projected-sessions bar is named.
- **ClassEconomySettings**: brass coin, hairline panels, sunken note, `duration-hec-2`.

Text a person reads is unchanged. New accessible names: "Actions for X", "Points for X", "Give the custom points for X", "RT, reading theory scores", "Select X", "Approve / Reject X's request", "Log spend for X", "Amount to deduct", "What was said or written", "New score for X", "Sessions held of those projected this month".

## Leftovers (each with its reason)
- Palette / `dark:` counts in the 12 branched files: the legacy branch (above).
- Bulk-mode checkbox on AssessmentStudentCard is 16-20 px. The whole card (>= 160 px) is the tap target that toggles selection. Making the checkbox itself 44 px, or wrapping it in a label, would change how the card's and the checkbox's click handlers fire (the card toggles too). Behaviour, not styling.
- The grid cards, the agenda rows and AttendanceMarking's session rows are `div`s with `onClick` and no keyboard access, same as before. Making them buttons moves handlers.
- Motion props in cross-area files are unchanged: PointFeedbackAnimation (scale 0.5, 0.8 s reward pop), the StudentActionPopover panel slide (12 px), the HUD meter width animation. The TeacherDashboard entrance was brought to 8 px / 240 ms.
- `truncate` kept on non-name text: the SAP recent-error line in legacy only (ivory wraps it).
- HUD chips show the last word of the name (`split(" ").slice(-1)`). That is text logic, unchanged; the full name is in the popover band.
- `/` for a signed-in teacher: StartupGuard redirects to `/dashboard` -> `/teacher/dashboard`, so AttendanceMarking is reachable only in the lab (it was checked there: list, mark view, save writes).
- On `/teacher/leaderboards` at 1280 the skill panel sits offset to the right (base too). The panel is `position: fixed` under a transformed ancestor, so it is placed relative to that ancestor. Positioning logic, not styled here.
- The floating classroom-tools button (Layout, R19) can sit over the panel's bottom-right corner at 320-375. The panel now scrolls its last row clear (`pb-16` below `sm`).
