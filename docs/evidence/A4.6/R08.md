## The flip and how `/tuition` is scoped

`IVORY_ROUTES` now lists, besides the A4.2 `/student/dashboard` and `/student`: `/student/assignments`, `/student/journal`, `/student/resources`, `/student/my-work`, `/student/videos`, `/student/record`, `/student/language-support`, `/student/lessons`, `/student/lessons/:id`, `/student/defend-level`, `/student/vocabulary`, `/vocabulary` (every student route in `App.tsx`), and `{ path: "/tuition", roles: ["student", "family"] }`.

The registry could only scope by route, so I extended it minimally: an `IvoryRoute` may carry `roles`; `isIvoryRoute(pathname, search, routes, complete, role)` matches such an entry only when the role is known and listed (while the role is still loading it does not match, so an admin never sees a flash of the new look). `IvoryScope` passes `useAuth().role`. Tests (`ivory-scope.test.mjs`, 45/0): `/tuition` student and family true; admin, teacher and unknown role false; `/students/:id/tuition`, `/admin`, `/teacher/dashboard`, `/family/dashboard`, `/schedule` false; unscoped entries ignore the role. Not listed (staff or parent area): `/schedule` (a student can reach it only by typing the URL; it would show the new bar over the old page), `/dashboard` (a redirect), `/family/dashboard`, `/`.
