## Prior balance computed in the browser (AdminTuitionList) - observed, not changed
`src/components/admin/AdminTuitionList.tsx` (base 44183c9 lines 90-100 and 120-126; lines 93-102 and 123-129 on this branch), used by `/tuition` and `/students/:id/tuition` as admin, does not take carry-in from calculate-tuition. It queries `invoices` for every earlier month (`.lt("month", month)`, selecting `student_id, total_amount, recorded_payment`), sums `recorded_payment - total_amount` per student into `priorBalance`, splits it into `carryInCredit` (positive part) and `carryInDebt` (negative part), then computes `finalPayable = invoice.total_amount + carryInDebt - carryInCredit`, `carryOutCredit/Debt = max(0, ±(recorded_payment - finalPayable))` and `balance = finalPayable - recorded_payment`; those drive the status badge, the filters and every Prior Bal./Payable/Balance figure on that screen. The comment above it says this mirrors the edge function's sum; it does not respect frozen bills, snapshots or settled_in_month beyond what the invoice rows hold, and the Finance tab (AdminTuitionListEnhanced via useLiveTuitionData) takes the same figures from calculate-tuition-bulk instead.
