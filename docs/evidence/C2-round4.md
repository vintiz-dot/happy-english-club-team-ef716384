## Mandatory

**R3-B1 - one pre-start limit.** Decision: the cap is raised to cover the whole capture window - the ONLY limit is `LEAST(max_duration_ms, started_at - capture_opens_at)`, i.e. the page may never claim audio from before the window opened (or longer than the window). Justification: the never-evidence rule (a chunk counts only if it starts at or after the server's `started_at`) already makes the length of pre-start audio irrelevant to evidence, so a numeric cap protected nothing and stranded a lesson after a half-hour outage; the window bound is what prevents back-dating. The expression is written once in the migration's trigger and once in `chunkMath.preStartLimitMs` (used by the engine), the fake implements it identically, and a unit case reads the migration's trigger text and asserts the expression (and that no `1800000` remains) plus the engine's values. Engine safety net: while the server has not accepted the offset the state is `starting` (never the red dot); `start_delayed` ("the server has accepted all of it") is said only after acceptance; three STATED refusals of the offset end it honestly - capture stops, microphones close, the row is abandoned (the Stop path ends), "This recording CANNOT be sent ... kept on this laptop and listed". The accepted flag is copied back into the in-memory record (no more re-sends).

**C3-a - the engine outlives the page.** `src/components/recorder/session.ts` owns the engine above the router (module level). `DailyRecorder` attaches on mount (re-using an existing engine for the same user) and detaches on unmount; while the engine is busy and no screen is attached, a fixed bar is drawn on whatever page is open (state in words, the red dot only while recording, Stop with a confirmation in every capturing state, "Open the recorder"). The engine is disposed only when idle with no screen, on a change of user, or through the sign-out guard (now registered by the session, so it works from any page). `Recorder.dispose()` while capturing first ends the capture properly (last audio stored, microphones closed, Stop queued); a test-only `kill()` keeps the old crash behaviour for the crash cases.
