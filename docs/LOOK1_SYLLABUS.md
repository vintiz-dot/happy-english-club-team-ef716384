# National Geographic Learning "Look 1": syllabus notes for building Mars class plays

Research done 25 Nov 2026 for the Plays tab (Mars class). Facts only, in our own words: titles, word lists, target sentence patterns and page numbers. No song lyrics, story texts or long passages are copied. Teacher-facing text for plays must cite pages and track numbers, never reproduce book text (same rule as the Oxford Discover / Reach Higher decks).

**Main source (official, complete for Look 1):** the publisher's Scope and Sequence PDF for the **American English** edition, <https://www.eltngl.com/assets/downloads/look_pro0000009082/lookame_ss.pdf> (called `[SS]` below). Every unit row below comes from its Look 1 page. Page numbers are the American-edition Student's Book pages.

## 2. Introductory unit and the 12 units

Wording of unit titles is NGL's. "Words" = Words box of the Words lesson; "Reading words" = extra words in the Reading lesson. Patterns are the Grammar box sentences (written here in our own spelling of the pattern; the book's own example names, e.g. Xi, Arneta, Victor, are character names).

### Intro unit "Look!" (p. 4, American edition)
- Words: numbers 1-10, colors, big, small.
- Language: Hello! What's your name? My name's ___. / How old are you? I'm six. / How are you? I'm fine, thanks.
- Source: `[SS]`. Songs, phonics, reading: **not listed** for this unit.

### Unit 1 Things for School (pp. 9-16)
- Words: bag, book, crayon, eraser, pen, pencil, pencil case, ruler.
- Reading words: poster, board, chair, desk.
- Grammar: **What's this? It's a ___.** (It's a pen.) / **Is it a ___? Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.**
- Reading: a classroom. Skills: listen and learn about school things in other countries; speak, draw, write about own school things.
- Phonics: letters Aa apple, Bb bag, Cc carrot, Dd desk.
- Value: take care of your school things.
- Song/chant: exists (Lesson 5) but title/content **not found**.

### Unit 2 Toys (pp. 17-24)
- Words: ball, bat, doll, game, kite, plane, teddy bear, train.
- Reading words: marble, favorite, fun.
- Grammar: **This is my ___.** (This is my train / my game.) / **Is this your ___? Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.**
- Reading: marbles. Skills: toys in other countries; own toys.
- Phonics: Ee elephant, Ff fish, Gg goat, Hh horse.
- Value: share your toys.
- Then: Function 1 Classroom language 1 (p. 25), School Trip 1 Toy Museum, Prague, Czech Republic (p. 26), Review 1 Units 1-2 (p. 28).

### Unit 3 People (pp. 29-36)
- Words: boy, girl, student, classmate, friend, man, woman, teacher.
- Reading words: museum, dinosaur, trip.
- Grammar: **He's a boy. He's my classmate. / She's a girl. She's my friend.** and **Is he a ___? Yes, he is. / No, he isn't. Is she your ___? Yes, she is.** Sample unit also shows "Who's this? This is ___." in the Lesson 2 chant (track 39) and "Who's that? A friend." in the video lesson.
- Reading: a girl on a school trip to a museum (track 42). Skills: famous paintings; people at school.
- Song (Lesson 5, tracks 44-47): about friends and classmates (title not shown; do not copy). Phonics: Ii insect, Jj jellyfish, Kk kiwi, Ll lamp (tracks 48-50). Video (Lesson 7) is a video of real children (Emilia, Kaitlyn, Marcel).
- Value: make friends at school.
- Sources: `[SS]`, Unit 3 sample (pp. 29-36 confirmed).

### Unit 4 My Family (pp. 37-44)
- Words: grandpa, grandma, dad, mom, uncle, aunt, baby, me, cousin.
- Reading words: middle, birthday party, birthday cake.
- Grammar: **I have a ___. / I don't have a ___.** (I have a grandpa. I don't have a sister.) and **His / Her name is ___.**
- Reading: a birthday party. Skills: cartoon families in other countries; own family.
- Phonics: Mm mom, Nn nose, Oo orange, Pp pencil.
- Value: give things to your friends.
- Then: Game 1 (p. 45), Reading Extra 1 "Day and Night" (p. 46), Review 2 Units 3-4 (p. 48).

### Unit 5 My Body (pp. 49-56)
- Words: leg, foot, mouth, eye, head, ear, arm, hand.
- Reading words: skeleton, hair, body, face.
- Grammar: **This is my ___. / These are my ___s.** (This is my head. These are my hands.) and **He has ___ hair. / She doesn't have ___ eyes.**
- Reading: face paint. Skills: festivals in other countries; the color of your eyes and hair.
- Phonics: Qq queen, Rr rabbit, Ss sofa, Tt table, Uu umbrella.
- Value: be active.

### Unit 6 Homes (pp. 57-64)
- Words: bathroom, bedroom, kitchen, living room, bed, cabinet, shower, TV.
- Reading words: house, clock, water.
- Grammar: **Where's the ___? It's in the ___.** and **The ___ is next to / on / under the ___.** (The table is next to the bed. The game is on the bed.)
- Reading: a bedroom under water. Skills: houses in other countries; own home.
- Phonics: Vv violin, Ww wall, Yy yogurt, Zz zebra (also Xx box).
- Value: play with your friends.
- Then: Function 2 Classroom language 2 (p. 65), School Trip 2 Animals on the Savanna, Africa (p. 66), Review 3 Units 5-6 (p. 68).

### Unit 7 My Town (pp. 69-76)
- Words: library, park, playground, store, street, swimming pool, town center, zoo.
- Reading words: real, model, tiny, flower.
- Grammar: **There's a ___ in / on the ___.** (There's a store on the street.) and **There are ___ next to the ___.** (There are houses next to the park.)
- Reading: a model town. Skills: towns and cities in other countries; own town.
- Phonics: CVC words with short a: bag, bat, jam, man, map.
- Value: love your town.

### Unit 8 On the Farm (pp. 77-84)
- Words: bee, bird, chicken, cow, dog, donkey, duck, sheep.
- Reading words: pen (animal pen), farmer, food.
- Grammar: **A ___ can ___. / A ___ can't ___.** (A dog can run. A cat can't swim.) and **Can you see the ___? Yes, I can.** / **Can a ___ ___? No, it can't.** (Can a duck talk?)
- Reading: a trip to a farm. Skills: farms in other countries; favorite farm animals.
- Phonics: CVC short e: bed, leg, pen, pet, yes.
- Value: be kind to animals.
- Then: Game 2 (p. 85), Reading Extra 2 "The Frog and the Butter..." (p. 86; title is cut off in my extract, very likely "The Frog and the Butterfly": **unconfirmed**), Review 4 Units 7-8 (p. 88).

### Unit 9 My Clothes (pp. 89-96)
- Words: dress, jeans, pants, shirt, shoes, skirt, socks, T-shirt.
- Reading words: scarecrow, happy, boots, hat, gloves.
- Grammar: **Are these your ___? Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.** (Are these your shoes? Are these your pants?) and **What color is his / her hair? It's ___. / What color are his eyes? They're ___.**
- Reading: a scarecrow. Skills: uniforms in other countries; own clothes.
- Phonics: CVC short i: big, bin, lip, sit, six.
- Value: wear clean clothes.

### Unit 10 Eat and Drink (pp. 97-104)
- Words: banana, bread, candy, lemon, milk, potato, rice, tomato, water.
- Reading words: lunch, tray, terrible, great.
- Grammar: **I like ___s. / I don't like ___.** (I like bananas. I don't like rice.) and **Do you like ___? Yes, I do. They're great. / They're OK.** / **No, I don't. It's terrible. / It's OK.** (Do you like milk?)
- Reading: lunch at school. Skills: food in other countries; favorite food.
- Phonics: CVC short o: dog, dot, fox, mop, nod.
- Value: eat good food.
- Then: Function 3 Classroom language 3 (p. 105), School Trip 3 Otavalo Market, Ecuador (p. 106), Review 5 Units 9-10 (p. 108).

### Unit 11 Beach Vacations (pp. 109-116)
- Words: beach, beach ball, boat, ice cream, sand, sandcastle, ocean, shell, sun hat.
- Reading words: mask, breathe, snorkel, flippers.
- Grammar: **There isn't a ___ / There aren't ___s ...** (There isn't a sun hat on my head. There aren't boats in the ocean.) and **Is there a ___ in the ___? Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. Are there ___s in the ___? Yes, there are.**
- Reading: a vacation at the beach. Skills: beaches in other countries; what you can do at the beach.
- Phonics: CVC short u: bus, cup, bug, jug, run.
- Value: play outside in the sun.

### Unit 12 Free Time (pp. 117-124)
- Words: fly a kite, make a cake, paint a picture, play a game, play soccer, read a book, sing a song, write a story.
- Reading words: hot, paper, swing.
- Grammar: **Let's ___. Great idea! / Let's ___. No!** (Let's fly a kite.) and **We're in / on the ___. We aren't at school.**
- Reading: a trip to the park. Skills: free-time activities in other countries; things you can do today.
- Phonics: review of CVC sounds (hat, red, kid, hot, sun).
- Value: use your time well.
- Then: Game 3 (p. 125), Reading Extra 3 "Colors" (p. 126), Review 6 Units 11-12 (p. 128). Look Further: One More Look (p. 129), BONUS School Trip "International Kite Festivals" (p. 130), BONUS Reading Extra "What's an Elephant?" (p. 132), BONUS Game (p. 134).

## 4. Notes for writing the plays

- Pattern per unit stays small: one or two question/answer pairs plus a short list of nouns. Plays should reuse the Words box exactly, and the Reading words only as extras.
- Recurring language across units (safe to recycle): What's this? It's a ___. / Is it a ___? Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. / This is my ___. / I have / I don't have ___. / Where's the ___? / There's / There are ___. / Can ___? / Do you like ___? / Let's ___.
- No story or song texts were available (copyright), so the plays should invent their own dialogue; cite "Look 1, Unit N, pp. X" only.
- Characters' names in the book (Xi, Emilia, Kaitlyn, Marcel, Dan, Kim, Jack, Lily, Arneta, Victor) are the publisher's; do not reuse them in decks.
