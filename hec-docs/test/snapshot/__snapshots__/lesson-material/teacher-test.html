HEC IELTS Speaking & Listening · Year 1 · Term 1

Term 1 check: Listening and Speaking

Lesson 12 · 60 minutes · Sun Class · Marked out of 30

Objectives
  • Listening: follow a conversation and a short talk, and note exact details.
  • Speaking: answer Part 1 questions with reasons and talk for one minute on a cue card.
01
Listening · Part 1

Booking a museum workshop

  • 6 marks
  • Heard twice

Questions 1–6

Complete the booking form. Write NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER for each answer.

  1. 1

    Workshop name: Making ______

    Answerpaper lanterns

  2. 2

    Day: ______

    AnswerSaturday

  3. 3

    Start time: ______

    Answer2.15 / quarter past two

  4. 4

    Number of children: ______

    Answer3

  5. 5

    Parent's surname: ______

    AnswerLam (L-A-M)

  6. 6

    Total price: ______ VND

    Answer360,000

For the teacherScript
  • Assistant:City Museum bookings, good afternoon.
  • Parent:Hello, I'd like to book the workshop where children make paper lanterns.
  • Assistant:Of course. We run it on Saturdays and Sundays, but Sunday is full this month.
  • Parent:Saturday is fine. What time does it start?
  • Assistant:Quarter past two, and it finishes at four.
  • Parent:There will be three children. The surname is Lam, L-A-M.
  • Assistant:That's a hundred and twenty thousand each, so three hundred and sixty thousand in total.
For the teacherMarking
  • One mark each. Accept 2:15 and 14:15. Spelling must be correct for question 5.

03
Speaking · Part 1

Interview: home and free time

  • 8 marks
  • 4 min

The examiner asks four questions. Answer each with a reason and a detail.

  1. 1

    Can you tell me about the place where you live?

  2. 2

    What do you usually do after school?

  3. 3

    Is there a hobby you would like to start? Why?

  4. 4

    Do you prefer spending free time alone or with friends?

For the teacherMarking (2 marks per answer)
  • 2 · A direct answer, a reason and a detail; easy to follow.
  • 1 · An answer and a reason, or needs one prompt.
  • 0 · One word, or does not answer the question.
  • Record the score; do not tell the student a band.
04
Speaking · Part 2

Cue card: a person who helped you

  • 12 marks
  • 1 min prep · 1 min talk

You have one minute to make notes, then talk for one minute.

Describe a person who helped you learn something. You should say:

  • who the person is
  • what they helped you learn
  • how they helped you
  • and explain why you remember it.

Writing space · 5 lines

For the teacherMarking (12 marks)
  • Content (4) · covers all four points with details.
  • Organisation (3) · a clear start, middle and end.
  • Language (3) · past tenses mostly correct; some linking words.
  • Fluency (2) · keeps going for the full minute.

Mistakes are how good English is made.
