HEC IELTS Speaking & Listening · Year 1 · Term 1

Getting to know you; how IELTS works

Lesson 1 · 120 minutes · Sun Class

Big Question
What makes a good first conversation with someone new?
Objectives
  • Introduce yourself and others with three linked details, not single words.
  • Know the shape of the IELTS Speaking and Listening papers and what examiners listen for.
  • Hear and mark word stress on two- and three-syllable words.
  • Answer Part 1 questions with a reason or an example, and score a partner fairly.
01
Warm-up

Find someone who…

  • 10 min
  • Whole class

Students stand and mingle. Each has a card of eight prompts and must find a different classmate for each one, then ask one follow-up question before writing a name.

How it runs

  1. 1.Model one exchange with a confident student, including the follow-up question.
  2. 2.Give four minutes of mingling; play quiet music and stop it at the end.
  3. 3.Students sit with the classmate they found most surprising and report one fact each.

Follow-up questions to model

  • “Oh really? How did you get into that?”
  • “What do you enjoy most about it?”
  • “How long have you been doing it?”
For the teacherWatch for
  • Single-word answers ("Yes." "Football."). Prompt with "Tell me more" rather than correcting.
  • Students who stay with a friend: give them a card with one prompt that only a new partner can answer.
02
Language

Talking about yourself in three steps

  • 15 min
  • Pairs

A strong Part 1 answer has three moves: a direct answer, a reason, and a detail or example. Build the pattern on the board, then let pairs practise with the prompts below.

MoveUseful languageExample
AnswerYes, I do / Not really / Mostly…Yes, I really enjoy it.
Reasonmainly because… / the thing is…mainly because it helps me relax after school.
Detailfor example… / last weekend I…Last Sunday I played for two hours with my cousins.
★ Support

Answer three questions with an answer and a reason, using the frames.

  • “Yes, I do, mainly because…”
  • “Not really. The thing is…”
★★ Core

Answer four questions with all three moves. Your partner ticks each move they hear.

★★★ Stretch

Answer, then add a contrast: what was different when you were younger?

  • “When I was younger, I used to…, but now…”
03
Pronunciation

Word stress: where the beat falls

  • 10 min
  • Whole class
  1. 1.Clap the beat of three names in the room; the loud clap is the stressed syllable.
  2. 2.Students sort the words into four stress patterns (Oo, oO, Ooo, oOo): hobby, describe, family, important, weekend, holiday, relax, computer.
  3. 3.Pairs say the words in a sentence of their own; the partner taps the stress.
For the teacherAnswer key
  • Oo: hobby, weekend (accept oO for weekend: both are heard).
  • oO: describe, relax.
  • Ooo: family, holiday.
  • oOo: important, computer.
04
IELTS Speaking · Part 1

Interview practice with peer scoring

  • 20 min
  • Pairs
  • Peer scoring

One student is the examiner and reads four questions; the other answers. After four minutes they swap. The examiner scores with the checklist and gives one specific piece of praise and one target.

Examiner's checklist

  • Answered the question that was asked
  • Gave a reason with every answer
  • Added a detail or example at least twice
  • Spoke for about 15 to 20 seconds each time
★ Support

Use the question card with picture prompts; answer with two moves.

★★ Core

Answer the four topic questions with three moves, without notes.

★★★ Stretch

Ask your partner one follow-up question of your own after each answer.

For the teacherScoring rubric (our paraphrase)
  • 3 · Answers flow: reason and example every time, few pauses.
  • 2 · Mostly extended; one or two short answers.
  • 1 · Short answers; needs prompting to add reasons.
  • Targets are targets: never tell a student what band they are.
05
Listening · Part 1

Joining a weekend club

  • 20 min
  • Individual

Students hear a short conversation at a sports centre twice. First listening: answer the questions. Second listening: check, then compare with a partner.

Questions 1–5

Write NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER, or tick the right box.

  1. 1

    The caller's first name is

    AnswerMinh

  2. 2

    Which activity does she want to join?

    • Swimming
    • Badminton
    • Climbing

    AnswerBadminton

  3. 3

    Sessions start at

    Answer8.30 / half past eight

  4. 4

    What should she bring to the first session?

    Answera water bottle

  5. 5

    The monthly fee is

    • 150,000 VND
    • 200,000 VND
    • 250,000 VND

    Answer200,000 VND

For the teacherListening script (read aloud or play by browser speech)
  • Receptionist:Good morning, Riverside Sports Centre. How can I help?
  • Caller:Hi, I'd like to join one of your weekend clubs. My name's Minh, M-I-N-H.
  • Receptionist:Lovely. We have swimming, badminton and climbing. Which one interests you?
  • Caller:I thought about climbing, but I think badminton suits me better.
  • Receptionist:Good choice. Sessions are on Saturdays and start at half past eight.
  • Caller:Do I need my own racket?
  • Receptionist:No, we lend those. Just bring a water bottle; the hall gets warm.
  • Caller:And how much is it?
  • Receptionist:It's two hundred thousand a month, or one fifty for students under twelve.
  • Note:question 5 is a distractor trap; the caller is fourteen.
06
Debate

This house believes first impressions are usually right

  • 25 min
  • Teams

Two teams of four. Each speaker has one minute. Proposition opens, opposition replies, then two minutes of open floor and a one-minute summary from each side.

Running order

  1. 1.Five minutes of preparation: each team lists three arguments and one example for each.
  2. 2.Speeches alternate: proposition 1, opposition 1, proposition 2, opposition 2.
  3. 3.Open floor: any speaker may stand to challenge a point.
  4. 4.Summaries, then the class votes by moving to one side of the room.

Language for debating

  • “I'd like to challenge that point.”
  • “That may be true, but…”
  • “Let me give you an example.”
  • “To sum up our case…”
★ Support

Give one argument with one example, reading from your notes.

★★ Core

Give two arguments and answer one challenge from the other team.

★★★ Stretch

Summarise both sides fairly before explaining why yours is stronger.

For the teacherManaging the room
  • Keep a visible timer; stop speakers kindly at one minute.
  • Praise good listening: a challenge that quotes the other team earns a point.
