Unit 6 – My Weekend / Cuối tuần của em

Hello everyone! This week we practise the past simple and talk about what we did at the weekend. Bố mẹ vui lòng nhắc con hoàn thành trước thứ Năm nhé.

Task 1 – Vocabulary (Từ vựng)
Copy each word three times in your notebook: visited, played, watched, cooked, cleaned.
Draw a small picture next to each word.
Say the words aloud to your parents – chú ý âm cuối -ed.
Task 2 – Reading
Read the story “Lan’s Saturday” on page 42 of the Student Book.
Underline every verb in the past simple.
Answer questions 1–5 on page 43 in full sentences.
Remember: regular verbs add -ed (play → played), but irregular verbs change (go → went, eat → ate). Đừng quên động từ bất quy tắc!
Task 3 – Writing

Write 6–8 sentences about your weekend. Use at least four verbs from the table below.

gowentI went to Hồ Tây with my grandma. eatateWe ate phở bò for breakfast. seesawI saw a big kite in the park. havehadWe had a picnic near the lake.
Task 4 – Listening

Listen to the audio and repeat after the speaker: Unit 6 listening track (https://example.test/hec/unit6-listening). Then record yourself reading Task 3 and send it to the teacher.
